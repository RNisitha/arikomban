About Magic Picturees

Magic Pictures is a dynamic and innovative film production house committed to bringing powerful stories to life through the magic of cinema. Driven by creativity, passion, and a dedication to quality, we strive to produce films that entertain, inspire, and leave a lasting impression on audiences.

We believe that storytelling has the power to connect people and evoke emotions. Our talented team combines artistic vision with modern filmmaking techniques to create visually stunning and emotionally engaging cinematic experiences. From gripping dramas and heartwarming comedies to thought-provoking documentaries, Magic Pictures is dedicated to delivering meaningful content that celebrates the richness of human experiences.

With every project, Magic Pictures aims to push creative boundaries and establish itself as a trusted name in the world of filmmaking.

Our Mission

To craft authentic, high-quality cinema that resonates with audiences worldwide, while nurturing new talent and pushing creative boundaries.

ARIKOMBAN

Directed by Mithun Maheshwaran

Kombayya, a courageous and respected tribal leader, shares a deep bond with his people and his childhood love, Kodhai. Raised under the guidance of the wise elder Muppar, he is destined to lead the united villages of Melthotti and Keelthotti. But soon after his marriage, a Forest Ranger wrongly arrests him, tearing the once-harmonious tribe apart. Grief, blame, and mistrust spread between the villages, while Kombayya returns home only to find his community divided and his life shattered.

In despair, Kombayya collapses after a drunken night, only for a hunted wild elephant to mysteriously die beside him. As fear grips the tribe, hidden truths, dangerous hunters, and looming authorities threaten their survival. Refusing to trust the Ranger again, Kombayya chooses to protect his people, forcing the village to make an impossible choice between unity and division. As danger closes in, the fate of the tribe, the truth behind the elephant's death, and Kombayya's love and leadership are put to the ultimate test.

The Vision Behind Arikomban

MM

Mithun Maheshwaran

Director · Lead Actor

Mithun Maheshwaran is a promising Indian actor known for his works in Tamil films. With a deep passion for acting, Mithun has starred in several notable films, including "Chennaiyil Orunaal," "Engeyum Eppothum," "Cocktail," "Sutupidika Utharavu," "Chikkiku Chikkikichu," and "Enga Kaatule Mazhai."

His dedication to the craft has not only made him a beloved figure in the industry but also inspired him to venture into direction. Driven by his love and enthusiasm for cinema, Mithun is set to make his Directorial debut with an intriguing plot and a talented crew, showcasing his multifaceted talent and commitment to the film industry.
